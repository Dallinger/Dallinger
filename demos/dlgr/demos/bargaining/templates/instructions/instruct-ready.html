Instructions

You will be paired with one other player. One of you is the seller and the other the buyer, and you bargain over the price of one item.

The seller has a cost and the buyer a value, in cents. Each of you sees only your own. A deal pays the seller the price minus their cost, and the buyer their value minus the price. You can make an offer at any time, change it, or accept the other player's offer.

If you haven't agreed when time runs out, neither of you earns anything.
